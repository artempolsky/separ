Technologies and engineering for urban mining.

SEPAR develops technologies for urban mining, from assessing the materials in existing buildings to creating new construction systems from recovered components.

SEPAR was founded in 2024 with Innovaud’s support and federal recognition of its project’s economic interest for Switzerland.

Building material inventory

From an address, estimate material quantities, embodied greenhouse-gas emissions, embodied energy and circularity. Use the inventory as environmental inputs to ESG reporting, identify structural reuse opportunities and connect recovered resources with secondary-material markets.

Applications
  • Quantify materials, CO₂e and embodied energy
  • Assess circularity and environmental ESG indicators
  • Identify structural reuse and secondary-material routes
Building assessment
From address to assessment

A building-level assessment brings together available records, material estimates and environmental indicators. Recovery options and priorities for further investigation provide a basis for the next project decision.

Portfolio strategy

Compare financial and environmental scenarios across the whole portfolio. Test how renewal timing, costs and recovery-market conditions affect projected expenditure, recovery value, embodied emissions and energy to guide investment and circularity priorities.

Applications
  • Compare portfolio-wide financial and environmental outcomes
  • Test sensitivity to renewal timing, costs and recovery markets
  • Prioritise investment and circularity across the portfolio
Material and portfolio study, 2026
Retraites Populaires

A three-building study covering concrete, steel, timber and glazing estimates, renovation, demolition and selective-disassembly scenarios, and associated material-production and disposal emissions.

Lending risk assessment

Assess building-related risks before financing. Examine renewal needs, demolition exposure and potential net material recovery value as part of technical due diligence.

Applications
  • Review renewal expenditure
  • Assess early-demolition exposure
  • Examine net recovery value
Lending assessment study, 2025
Triodos Bank N.V. Deutschland

Study of building-related lending risks and material recovery value in the context of financing requirements. The analytical scope covers potential resale proceeds, dismantling, transport and preparation costs.

Territorial urban-mining strategy

Develop an urban-mining strategy for a municipality or region. Connect material resources and future flows with construction demand, logistics and recovery infrastructure.

Applications
  • Map future material resources
  • Plan collection and processing capacity
  • Connect supply with local demand
Territorial resource study, 2025–2026
Direction générale de l’environnement, Canton of Vaud

Territorial material-resource study covering 234,775 building records in the Canton of Vaud. The analytical scope includes material-resource maps, geodata and demolition-flow scenarios to 2035.

SEPAR Structures

SEPAR develops prefabricated structural components from concrete elements recovered during demolition.

Research objectives include design flexibility, reduced environmental impact and the economic feasibility of prefabrication using recovered concrete.

SEPAR publishes field observations, research milestones and public tools. Technical development is conducted through research and industrial collaborations.

Concrete reuse advisory

Our concrete-recovery research and structural testing inform project-specific advice: reuse assessments, deconstruction and cutting plans, contractor selection and process coordination, and briefs for tenders and design competitions.

Development roadmap

    TRL 1: basic principles observed. SEPAR’s first stage combined initial studies with market research.

    Recovery and supply-chain research

    First concrete elements recovered with Orllati Group. Structural concept developed for the prototype.

    Production-chain costing

    Cost assessment covering building and material surveys, cutting and recovery, transport, storage, preparation, fabrication and assembly. Data gaps and practical constraints identified along the chain.

    TRL 3: experimental proof of concept. SEPAR built and tested the production sequence through fabrication and prototype assembly.

    A prototype production line developed with Orllati Group: preparing recovered concrete, fabricating components and assembling the structural prototype.

SEPAR Demolition Map

Public access to SEPAR’s urban-mining research and technology.

The platform documents Switzerland’s changing building stock, preserves digital records of demolished buildings and makes material assessments available for research, planning and resource recovery.

Monitor demolition

Official building records, geodata and imagery are brought together in a daily update. AI-assisted image review checks building visibility.

Preserve building knowledge

Photographs, maps and reconstructed outlines retain evidence of buildings that have disappeared, forming a spatial archive for research.

Assess material resources

SEPAR’s assessment engine estimates material stocks and urban-mining potential, helping identify opportunities for component recovery and recycling.

Demolition dates refer to permit issuance, typically leaving around two weeks to two months before actual demolition, while delayed data updates make the timing indicative.

Supported through Viva Vaud.

Sources & dates

Federal Register of Buildings and Dwellings (RBD / GWR); swisstopo / geo.admin.ch; Swiss Federal Office of Energy (SFOE) solar facade data; OpenStreetMap contributors; Esri World Imagery; Google Maps / Street View; SEPAR monitoring and analysis.

Historical records are interpreted according to their source and registered demolition year. Daily additions reflect newly detected register entries. Permit dates, register updates and actual demolition dates can differ.
